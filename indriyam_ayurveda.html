A few helpful details

Good to know.

How do I order a product?

Choose a product and send an enquiry. Indriyam can confirm availability, final pricing, delivery terms and payment instructions before an order is accepted.

How do I book a consultation?

Send a request through the contact form with your preferred service and contact details. The team can follow up to confirm availability and explain the process.

Are online courses available?

Course topics, format, schedule, fees and access instructions will be shared on each course listing once your offerings are finalised.

Can Ayurvedic products replace medical care?

No. Product and educational information should not be treated as a substitute for diagnosis or care from a qualified medical professional. Seek appropriate medical advice for health concerns.
